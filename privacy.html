Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Overview

Personal Assistant is a privately operated application intended for personal productivity and household organization. It is not offered as a public service.

2. Google Account Access

With user authorization, the application accesses Google services through OAuth 2.0, including:

  • Gmail: reading, searching, and sending email.
  • Google Calendar: viewing and managing calendar events.
  • Google Drive: searching and reading authorized files.

Access is limited to the permissions granted by each user.

3. Data Processing

The application runs on privately managed infrastructure. Google account data may be retrieved and processed to fulfill requests initiated by authorized users or their configured automations.

Relevant information may be transmitted to an external AI service provider for processing when necessary to fulfill those requests.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Authentication credentials and application data may be stored on the privately managed system.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Data is shared with service providers only as necessary to operate the assistant and fulfill authorized requests.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Application data may be retained locally as part of configuration, conversation history, or operational logs. Authorized users can request deletion of locally stored application data from the system administrator.

6. Security

The application uses OAuth 2.0 for Google authorization. Access to the application is restricted to authorized users. Reasonable measures are used to protect stored credentials and application data.

7. Revoking Access

Users can revoke Google account access through their Google Account security settings.

8. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application's functionality or data handling.
