Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Overview

Personal Assistant is a private productivity application intended exclusively for authorized users.

Information Accessed

With user permission, the application accesses Google account data, including email, calendar information, and files, to provide its requested functionality.

Data Use and Sharing

Information is used to fulfill authorized user requests and configured automations. Relevant data may be processed by external service providers, including AI processing services, when necessary to operate the application.

User data is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and Security

Application data and authentication credentials may be stored on privately managed systems. Access is restricted to authorized users.

Retention and Deletion

Application data may be retained in configuration, conversation history, and operational logs. Authorized users can request deletion of locally stored data from the application administrator.

Revoking Access

Google account access can be revoked at any time through Google Account settings.
